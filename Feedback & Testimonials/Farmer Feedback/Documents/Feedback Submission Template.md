## Publishing guidance

- Publish feedback only after receiving the person's permission.
- Do not publish phone numbers, addresses, signatures, or other private details.
- Keep the person's words accurate; correct spelling only when the meaning is unchanged.
- Add a photograph only when permission to publish the photograph has also been obtained.
- Do not invent testimonials or present sample text as real feedback.
